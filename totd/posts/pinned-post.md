Thank you for your interest in radx.io the codable math game®! This is the early edition release, offered in two formats that share the same underlying rules and gameplay.

- The Dice + Card Edition features custom-designed and 3D-printed dice (aka "bits") and ciphers, emphasizing the physical qualities of play: weight, texture, and precision.

- The Card Only Edition translates the same playing mechanics into an elegant, fully card-based format that is lighter, more portable, and easy to bring anywhere.

For the foreseeable future, both editions will be produced in small, carefully managed production runs using professionally crafted 3D-printed components and high-quality cards printed in the United States. These early editions are offered at founder pricing, below future retail.

As with any early production, subtle variations may be present and reflect the hands-on nature of this release. If demand grows significantly, this phase will serve as proof of concept for a future transition to traditional manufacturing, while preserving the design intent and learning experience that define radx.io the codable math game.®
